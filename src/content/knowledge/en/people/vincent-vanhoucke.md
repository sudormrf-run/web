## Career Transition (2024)

In 2024, Vincent Vanhoucke left Google after 17 years to join Waymo. In his Medium post "Whymo?", he explained his reasons:

1. **Health Issues**: Health concerns requiring time and focus
2. **Team Maturity**: Google DeepMind Robotics team had grown sufficiently for leadership transition
3. **New Challenge**: After 8 years building "the best research team on robotics and AI today," seeking new frontiers

### Why Waymo
- Developing safer and smarter autonomous vehicles using foundation models
- While robotics research focuses on ~70% performance, Waymo tackles 99%+ safety challenges
- "The long tail of difficulty is increasingly more about common sense reasoning than about low-level planning and control"
