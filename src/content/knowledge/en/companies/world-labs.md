## Overview

World Labs is an AI startup founded in January 2024, focused on developing Spatial Intelligence - AI systems that can perceive, generate, reason about, and interact with 3D environments. The company achieved unicorn status approximately 4 months after founding. It emerged from stealth mode in September 2024.

|------|---------|
| Headquarters | San Francisco, CA |
| Founded | January 2024 |

## Core Technology: Spatial Intelligence

### What is Spatial Intelligence?

```
Traditional AI: 2D images/text → 2D outputs

Spatial Intelligence: 2D/3D inputs → 3D world understanding
                     ↓
                     - 3D scene generation
                     - Physics understanding
                     - Spatial reasoning
```

### Applications

- **Robotics**: 3D world understanding for manipulation
- **Gaming**: Procedural 3D world generation
- **Creative Tools**: AI-assisted 3D design
- **AR/VR**: Immersive environment creation

---

## Products

### Marble (2025)

First commercial product for 3D world generation:

- **Launch**: Limited beta in September 2025, general availability in November 2025
- Text/image/video/panorama prompts → 3D worlds
- Physics-aware generation
- Chisel Editor: AI-native 3D editing tools
- Hybrid 3D Editor: Block out spatial structures, AI fills in visual details
- Export to Gaussian splat, mesh, or video formats
- Compatible with Vision Pro and Quest 3 VR headsets
- Tools for artists, designers, and developers
- Freemium subscription model
