## Overview

World Labs is an AI startup founded in early 2024, focused on developing Spatial Intelligence - AI systems that can perceive, generate, reason about, and interact with 3D environments. The company achieved unicorn status within months of founding.

## Core Technology: Spatial Intelligence

### What is Spatial Intelligence?

```
Traditional AI: 2D images/text → 2D outputs

Spatial Intelligence: 2D/3D inputs → 3D world understanding
                     ↓
                     - 3D scene generation
                     - Physics understanding
                     - Spatial reasoning
```

### Applications

- **Robotics**: 3D world understanding for manipulation
- **Gaming**: Procedural 3D world generation
- **Creative Tools**: AI-assisted 3D design
- **AR/VR**: Immersive environment creation

---

## Products

### Marble (2024)

First commercial product for 3D world generation:

- Text/image/video prompts → 3D worlds
- Physics-aware generation
- Interactive editing capabilities
