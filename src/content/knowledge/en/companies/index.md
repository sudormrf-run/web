## Big Tech

### [Tesla](./tesla)
Electric vehicle company developing the Optimus humanoid robot. Leveraging autonomous driving AI and manufacturing capabilities, operates a large-scale teleoperation team and deploys in its own factories (dogfooding). Target price $20,000-30,000 for mass production.

### [Google DeepMind](./google-deepmind)
Pioneer of VLA (Vision-Language-Action) models. Leading robotics AI research from RT-1, RT-2, RT-X to Gemini Robotics. Collaborating with 33 research labs through the Open X-Embodiment project.

### [NVIDIA](./nvidia)
Core infrastructure provider for robotics AI. Building full-stack solutions with Isaac (development platform), Omniverse (digital twin), Cosmos (World Model), and GR00T (humanoid model). Partnering with major humanoid companies.

## Startups - Foundation Model

### [Physical Intelligence](./physical-intelligence)
General-purpose robot foundation model startup founded by team from Google DeepMind and Stanford. pi0 model (3.3B) trained on 51 tasks across 20 robot configurations using Flow Matching. Largest robotics funding ever ($400M+), $2.4B valuation.

### [Skild AI](./skild-ai)
Founded by Carnegie Mellon professors (Deepak Pathak, Abhinav Gupta). Developing general-purpose robot "brain" through large-scale simulation-based learning. $300M funding, $1.5B valuation.

### [Generalist AI](./generalist)
Team from OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Boston Dynamics. Claims discovery of first scaling laws for robotics with 270,000+ hours of real physical interaction data. Developing GEN-0 model with Harmonic Reasoning architecture.

### [Covariant](./covariant)
Logistics automation AI company founded by Pieter Abbeel (UC Berkeley). Developing general-purpose robot AI with RFM-1 (8B) model. Active deployment to 26+ customers. Key founders joined Amazon in 2024.

## Startups - Humanoid

### [Figure AI](./figure)
Leading humanoid robotics company founded by Brett Adcock. Progressed to Figure 03 with in-house Helix VLA model. Achieved 90,000+ parts handling at BMW factory. $1.7B+ funding, $39B valuation.

### [1X Technologies](./1x)
Norway-based humanoid startup. Differentiated by NEO (30kg lightweight humanoid) and World Model-based evaluation/learning system. Invested by OpenAI and Samsung. $240M+ funding.

### [Boston Dynamics](./boston-dynamics)
Pioneer in dynamic locomotion robots, spun off from MIT in 1992. Developed Atlas, Spot, Stretch. Transitioning from Classical Robotics to Physical AI with Large Behavior Model (LBM). Acquired by Hyundai in 2020.

### [Sunday Robotics](./sunday-robotics)
Founded by ACT and Diffusion Policy paper authors (Tony Zhao, Cheng Chi). "Zero Robot Data" approach using $200 gloves to collect human motion data without robot data. Targeting home market with Memo robot.

## Startups - China

### [Galbot](./galbot)
Achieved first large-scale commercial deployment in VLA with "Synthetic First, Real Data as a Complement" philosophy. GraspVLA model achieves 82% on LIBERO zero-shot. Operating in 10+ cities with 30+ unmanned pharmacy warehouses.

### [Unitree](./unitree)
Low-cost, high-performance robot hardware manufacturer. Produces G1 humanoid ($16,000), H1 humanoid (3.3m/s world fastest), Go2 quadruped robots. Reinforcement learning-based control. Estimated ~$7B valuation.

---

## Open Source / Community

### [HuggingFace](./huggingface)
Democratizing robotics AI through LeRobot project. SmolVLA (450M) model runs on MacBook. 487+ community datasets, supports low-cost hardware (~$300) like SO100 and LeKiwi.

---

## AI Research / Spatial Intelligence

### [World Labs](./world-labs)
Spatial Intelligence startup founded by Fei-Fei Li (ImageNet creator). Developing AI for 3D world perception/generation/reasoning. Achieved unicorn status within months of founding with $230M+ funding.
